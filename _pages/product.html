The problem

Creator & affiliate marketing is a $52B channel. The tools around it are broken in three ways.

01
Discovery starts from strangers
Brands buy generic creator databases and cold-pitch people who've never heard of them — while their most authentic partners, their own customers, stay invisible.
02
The workflow is in pieces
Sourcing in one tool, outreach in a spreadsheet, contracts in email, payouts in a third system — and attribution nowhere.
03
Trust is unproven
“Vetted” scores are gameable, creators ghost, and finance can't see what any of it returned. The channel can't defend its own budget.
